TRAUMA

Online Therapy for Trauma in Massachusetts

Steady, respectful support after difficult or overwhelming experiences.

Healing happens at your pace.

Difficult experiences, whether recent or long ago, can continue to affect how safe, connected, or in control you feel. Therapy offers a place to make sense of those effects without pressure to share more than you're ready to.

You might consider reaching out if you notice:

  • Memories or reminders that feel intrusive or overwhelming
  • Feeling on guard, jumpy, or easily startled
  • Avoiding people, places, or topics
  • Difficulty trusting others or feeling close to them
  • Changes in mood, sleep, or concentration after an event

HOW THERAPY CAN HELP

What to expect.

We'll start by building a sense of safety and stability, including skills to manage strong reactions, before deciding together how and when to explore harder material.

You stay in control of the pace. The goal is to help you feel more grounded in daily life and in your relationships.

Every person's experience is different, and therapy works best as a collaboration. We'll set goals together and adjust as we go.
