MAKING A REFERRAL

A straightforward referral process.

  1. Share our information

    The simplest path is to give the client or family this website. They can reach out directly through the contact page.

  2. Or contact me directly

    Use the contact form with the subject "Professional Referral." Please do not include patient names or other identifying health information. I'll reply to arrange a secure way to share details.

  3. Coordination of care

    With the client's written authorization, I'm glad to coordinate with referring providers.

FIT

When a referral may not be a good fit.

Connect Therapy is an outpatient telehealth practice. It does not provide emergency, crisis, or in-person services. Clients who need immediate safety support or a higher level of care should be directed to appropriate emergency or specialty services. In a crisis, call 911 or call or text 988.
