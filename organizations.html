Partnering with Massachusetts Schools & Organizations

Counseling-informed collaboration for schools, districts, agencies, and employers.

EDUCATION & MENTAL HEALTH

Experience in both worlds.

With a doctorate in education and experience in private school, residential, outreach, and family counseling settings, Dr. Bruzzese understands how student, family, and staff well-being connect to learning and day-to-day operations.

Connect Therapy welcomes conversations with Massachusetts schools, districts, community agencies, and employers about how we might work together.

AREAS FOR DISCUSSION

Ways we might collaborate.

Schools & Districts

Consultation with student services teams and a clear referral pathway for students and families who may benefit from outpatient telehealth counseling.

Agencies & Community Organizations

Referral relationships and coordination for clients who are a good fit for telehealth counseling.

Employers

Educational conversations on topics such as stress and well-being, scoped to your team's needs.

Scope, availability, and arrangements are discussed individually. Educational and consulting services are separate from therapy and do not create a counselor–client relationship.
