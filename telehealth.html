ELIGIBILITY

Who can use telehealth with Connect Therapy?

Located in Massachusetts

You must be physically in Massachusetts at the time of each session, whether at home, at work, or at school.

A private space

Choose a quiet, private location where you can speak openly without being overheard.

Clinically appropriate

Telehealth is a good fit for many people, but not all. We'll talk together about whether virtual care meets your needs.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST SESSION

Getting set up.

  • A phone, tablet, or computer with a camera and microphone
  • A reliable internet connection (a private network rather than public Wi-Fi when possible)
  • Headphones for extra privacy
  • A few minutes before your session to log in and check your audio and video

Telehealth is not appropriate for emergencies. If you are in crisis, call 911 or call or text 988.
