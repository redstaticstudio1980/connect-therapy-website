OUR PRACTICE

Connect. Reflect. Grow.

Connect Therapy, PLLC is a Massachusetts counseling practice offering secure, confidential telehealth sessions. Therapy can help you navigate challenges, develop new skills, and create a more fulfilling life.

Our approach is eclectic, so methods and strategies are tailored to each person rather than requiring anyone to fit one particular model. The goal is a safe, respectful, and supportive environment where clients can explore challenging issues and build skills they can use beyond the therapy session.

  • Children, adolescents, adults, couples, and families
  • 100% telehealth, with evening availability
  • MassHealth and Blue Cross Blue Shield accepted (plan details)
