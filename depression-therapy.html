DEPRESSION

Online Therapy for Depression in Massachusetts

A supportive space to understand low mood and take steps toward feeling more like yourself.

You don't have to work through it alone.

Depression can make everyday tasks feel heavy and can affect how you think, sleep, and connect with others. Counseling offers a place to talk openly and to identify small, realistic steps forward.

You might consider reaching out if you notice:

  • Persistent sadness, emptiness, or irritability
  • Losing interest in things you used to enjoy
  • Changes in sleep, energy, or appetite
  • Pulling away from friends, family, or activities
  • Feeling stuck, hopeless, or hard on yourself

HOW THERAPY CAN HELP

What to expect.

Together we'll explore what's contributing to how you feel, reconnect with what matters to you, and build routines and coping skills that support your well-being.

If you ever have thoughts of harming yourself, please call or text 988 or call 911. Those services are available 24/7.

Every person's experience is different, and therapy works best as a collaboration. We'll set goals together and adjust as we go.
