FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What to know before getting started.

Are sessions offered in person?

Connect Therapy is a telehealth practice. Sessions are conducted virtually.

Do I need to be in Massachusetts?

Yes. You need to be physically located in Massachusetts at the time of each session. Read more about telehealth.

Do you accept insurance?

Connect Therapy accepts MassHealth and Blue Cross Blue Shield. Coverage varies by plan, so please confirm your specific plan. See Insurance & Fees.

Who do you work with?

Children, adolescents, adults, couples, and families, when telehealth is clinically appropriate.

What is your therapeutic approach?

I use an eclectic approach, drawing from different therapeutic methods based on each client's needs, goals, and circumstances.

Do you offer evening appointments?

Yes. Evening hours are available to help accommodate work, school, and family schedules.

What should I do in an emergency?

Connect Therapy does not provide emergency or crisis services through this website. If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a medical emergency, call 911. In the U.S., you may also call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
