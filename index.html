ABOUT THE PRACTICE

A versatile perspective on counseling.

Ralph Bruzzese, Ed.D., LMHC

With experience across mental health and educational settings, I bring a practical and adaptable perspective to therapy. My background includes private school, residential, office-based, outreach, and family counseling environments.

My therapeutic approach is eclectic, allowing methods and strategies to be tailored to the individual rather than requiring the individual to fit one particular model.

The goal is to foster a safe, respectful, and supportive environment where clients can explore challenging issues and develop skills they can use beyond the therapy session.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What to know before getting started.

Are sessions offered in person?

Connect Therapy is a telehealth practice. Sessions are conducted virtually.

Who do you work with?

Services are available for children, adolescents, and adults when telehealth is clinically appropriate.

What is your therapeutic approach?

I use an eclectic approach, drawing from different therapeutic methods based on each client's needs, goals, and circumstances.

Do you offer evening appointments?

Yes. Evening hours are available to help accommodate work, school, and family schedules.

What should I do in an emergency?

Connect Therapy does not provide emergency or crisis services through this website. If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a medical emergency, call 911. In the U.S., you may also call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
