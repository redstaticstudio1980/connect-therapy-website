MY APPROACH

Therapy tailored to you.

My therapeutic approach is eclectic, so methods and strategies are tailored to the individual rather than requiring the individual to fit one particular model.

The goal is to foster a safe, respectful, and supportive environment where clients can explore challenging issues and develop skills they can use beyond the therapy session.
